Scientific Word Generator

Scientific words are typically combinations of Greek or Latin words. Because science is pioneering new territory, these words are often defined for things that we know nothing about, and so get named after some of the most obvious characteristics -- which can sound humorous. For example, "cephalopod," meaning "head legs." This tool lets you create your own scientific words. Enjoy!
